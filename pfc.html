Before you ship

What worries importers most, answered

These are the questions buyers ask us again and again.

  • Will the price grow after my goods arrive?

    It is the fear we hear most. So ask every forwarder for the full price to your door, in writing. Ask us too. A quote is free, and we reply within 24 hours.

  • What if my suppliers deliver on different days?

    Send them all to our warehouse. We charge no receiving fee, and storage is free for the first 30 days. Then we combine everything into one shipment.

  • Who clears customs?

    We prepare the export papers on every shipment. On air and sea freight we clear customs at both ends. On DDP, we also pay the duty.

  • What if goods are lost or damaged?

    By law, a carrier pays by weight, not by value. We offer cargo cover for an extra fee. Ask before you ship.
